# Amazon Coupons & Subscribe & Save: Check What You’ll Actually Pay

**Before buying, check three things: the purchase option you selected, whether the coupon applies to your order, and what happens after the first delivery.** A low first-order price is not a promise that future deliveries will cost the same.

## Start with the purchase option

A one-time purchase and a Subscribe & Save order are different commitments. Subscribe & Save schedules repeat deliveries. Amazon’s terms say the subscription continues until you cancel, and details such as price, discounts, availability, shipping charges, and seller can change over time.<a href="#source-1" aria-label="Source 1">[1]</a>

Before comparing prices, decide whether you want the product once or expect to reorder it. A subscription can be useful for something you regularly use, but it is not automatically the cheapest or most convenient choice for every purchase.

## Check the coupon’s terms—not just the badge

Read the offer attached to the exact product and purchase option you selected. Check whether it requires Subscribe & Save, applies to a particular quantity or variant, or has an eligibility restriction.

Amazon offers Subscribe & Save-specific coupons that can be redeemed on the first delivery of a new subscription.<a href="#source-4" aria-label="Source 4">[4]</a> **Do not assume a first-delivery coupon repeats on later orders.** Other promotions can have different terms.

If the offer requires you to select or clip a coupon, do that before completing the purchase. Then look for the discount in the order summary. If it is missing, stop and check the terms rather than assuming it will appear later.

Don’t simply add percentage badges together to estimate your savings. Review the discounts actually shown for your order.

## Compare the right prices

Keep these separate:

- **One-time purchase:** What would this order cost without starting a subscription?
- **First subscription delivery:** What does the order summary show after the discounts you qualify for?
- **Later deliveries:** Which savings are ongoing, and which belong only to the initial offer?

Amazon can change subscription prices and discounts.<a href="#source-1" aria-label="Source 1">[1]</a> It also sends a reminder before a delivery showing the products, price, and applicable discount.<a href="#source-5" aria-label="Source 5">[5]</a> Treat that reminder as a shopping decision, not an email to ignore.

Amazon describes savings of up to 15% on eligible subscriptions when you receive five or more subscriptions in one auto-delivery.<a href="#source-5" aria-label="Source 5">[5]</a> That is not a promise that every item or order qualifies. Check the savings shown for your delivery, and don’t add products you don’t need just to reach a threshold.

## A quick checkout checklist

Before placing the order, confirm:

- The title, photo, size, pack count, shade or other variant describe the same item.
- You know who is selling it.
- You selected one-time purchase or Subscribe & Save deliberately.
- The coupon and any eligibility conditions match your order.
- The order summary includes the expected discount, shipping charges, and tax information.
- The quantity and delivery timing work for your household.
- You understand which offers apply only to the first delivery.

If something does not match the deal you expected, don’t complete the purchase until you understand the difference.

## Review repeat deliveries before they are processed

Use Amazon’s subscription-management page to review your upcoming order, delivery frequency, quantity, and available changes. Amazon says you can adjust subscriptions, skip deliveries, or cancel.<a href="#source-5" aria-label="Source 5">[5]</a>

Check the **“Last day to update this order”** date shown for your subscription. Make changes before that deadline; canceling the subscription does not necessarily stop an order already in the shipping process.<a href="#source-2" aria-label="Source 2">[2]</a>

To cancel, Amazon’s published instructions are to open **Your Subscribe & Save Items**, choose **Subscriptions**, select the product or **Edit**, then **Cancel subscription** and confirm **Cancel my subscription**.<a href="#source-2" aria-label="Source 2">[2]</a> Labels may vary between the app and website.

## Common questions

### Do I need Amazon Prime?

Amazon says Prime membership is not required to use Subscribe & Save.<a href="#source-5" aria-label="Source 5">[5]</a> Still review the shipping charges and offer conditions shown for your particular order rather than assuming every purchase has identical benefits.

### Will my next delivery cost the same?

Not necessarily. Prices and discounts can change, and an introductory coupon may not apply again. Review the reminder and subscription details before the next order’s update deadline.<a href="#source-1" aria-label="Source 1">[1]</a><a href="#source-2" aria-label="Source 2">[2]</a><a href="#source-4" aria-label="Source 4">[4]</a>

### Should I subscribe just because the first price is lower?

Only if the arrangement works for you. Compare the actual order costs and decide whether you want repeat deliveries. Saving on one purchase is less useful if you later receive quantities you don’t need.

## Keep the deal useful—not just cheap

The best purchase for you is the right product, in a useful quantity, at a price you understand. A large discount badge cannot answer those questions for you.
