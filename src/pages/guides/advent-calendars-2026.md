## A few things worth knowing before you buy

**Buy the LEGO ones first.** They sell out every single year, and the licensed themes go before the generic ones.

**Check what is actually inside a beauty calendar.** The good ones list their contents. If a calendar will not tell you what is in it, that usually means the contents are not the draw.

**One calendar per kid, or buy a two pack.** Sharing an advent calendar does not work. Nobody has ever made that work.
